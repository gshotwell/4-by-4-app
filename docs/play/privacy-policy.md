# 4x4 Coach privacy policy

_Last updated: 16 November 2026_

4x4 Coach is a workout timer that reads your heart rate from a Bluetooth chest strap. It is built by an individual developer, not a company.

**What the app handles.** While you use it, the app receives your heart rate from your strap and stores, on your phone only: your workout settings (zones, durations, max heart rate), the name and Bluetooth address of your paired strap, and a record of each workout (timestamps and heart-rate readings). Workout records are kept as CSV files in the app's storage.

**What leaves your phone.** Nothing, unless you choose:
- **Health Connect.** If you turn on "Save to Health Connect", finished workouts (an exercise session and its heart-rate samples) are written to Health Connect on your phone, where apps you have authorised can read them. The app does not read anything from Health Connect.
- **Sharing a CSV.** If you tap "Share CSV", the workout file goes to whichever app you pick.
- **Music control.** The "next song" option sends a standard media-button command to your music app. No data is included.

The app has no account, no analytics, no ads, no crash reporting and no internet access. The developer never receives your data.

**Deleting your data.** Clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Data you exported to Health Connect can be deleted in Health Connect's settings.

**Children.** The app is intended for adults.
